 
Evaluation  ## Overview

Submissions are evaluated as **reconstruction algorithms**, not predictions. Each submission is executed against hidden datasets and must reconstruct the original input data from the provided one-dimensional values.

Evaluation is performed through a **multi-stage validation pipeline**. A submission must pass *all stages* to be considered for the Grand Prize.

---

## 1. Evaluation Framework

Each submitted algorithm is run on a hidden dataset:

Z Ôêê R^(N ├ù 1)

and must produce a reconstruction:

X_hat Ôêê R^(N ├ù D_hat)

The true data X Ôêê R^(N ├ù D) is never exposed.

---

## 2. Evaluation Stages

### Stage 1 ÔÇö Execution & Validity
The submission must:
- execute successfully
- complete within resource limits
- output valid numeric values (no NaN/Inf)
Failure at this stage results in submission disqualification

---

### Stage 2 ÔÇö Structural Validation
The output must satisfy:
- Correct row count (N)
- Correct dimensionality (D)
Dimensionality is not disclosed to participants.
Submissions with incorrect shape are rejected.

---

### Stage 3 ÔÇö Record Alignment
Reconstruction must be row/record aligned:
- Row i must correspond to input row i
- Permuted outputs are not accepted
This prevents distribution-level or generative solutions from qualifying.

---

### Stage 4 ÔÇö Reconstruction Accuracy
For valid submissions, reconstruction error is computed using a standardized error metric:

e_ij = |x_hat_ij - x_ij| / s_j

where s_j is a robust scale estimate for feature j.

Primary metric:

SRMSE = sqrt( (1 / (N┬ÀD)) * sum(e_ij^2) )

Additional constraints may include:
- High percentile error thresholds
- Feature-wise correlation checks
- Row-level error consistency
A submission must meet strict predefined thresholds to proceed.

---

### Stage 5 ÔÇö Baseline Separation
To prevent random or trivial wins, submissions are compared against and must outperform naive baseline strategies:
- Random guesses
- Distribution matching
- Constant outputs
A valid solution must outperform these baselines by a statistically significant margin.

---

### Stage 6 ÔÇö Latent Dependence Verification
Submissions must demonstrate that outputs depend on the provided inputs.

This is tested by:
- Permuting input rows
- Evaluating output consistency under transformation

Valid solutions must satisfy:

f(PZ) Ôëê P f(Z)

Submissions that ignore inputs or produce fixed outputs are disqualified.
---

### Stage 7 ÔÇö Generalization
Top submissions are evaluated on additional hidden datasets not used during initial scoring.

To qualify, performance must:
- remain consistent across datasets
- not degrade significantly

This prevents overfitting to a single dataset.

---

### Stage 8 ÔÇö Code Review
Finalist submissions undergo manual review to verify:
- the method is legitimate
- the method must not exploit Kaggle system behavior or other unintended signals
- results are reproducible

---

## 3. Leaderboard Policy
- During the competition, submissions receive limited or no scoring feedback
- Final rankings are determined using private evaluation datasets

This prevents iterative probing of the hidden data.

---

## 4. Winning Criteria

To win the Full Reconstruction Challenge, a submission must:

- Infer the correct dimensionality
- Reconstruct individual records with high accuracy
- Demonstrate dependence on the input data
- Generalize to unseen datasets
- Pass all validation and review stages

---

## 5. Independent Audit & Verification

To ensure maximum credibility and transparency:

- Finalist submissions may be reviewed by **independent third-party experts** or auditors.
- The evaluation process, scoring methodology, and validation results may be **replicated independently** to confirm outcomes.
- Additional hidden datasets may be introduced during audit to verify robustness.
- Code and methodology must be sufficiently clear to allow **independent reproduction of results**.

The purpose of independent verification is to ensure that:

- Any winning solution represents a genuine reconstruction method  
- No unintended evaluation artifacts or loopholes were exploited  
- Results are robust, reproducible, and scientifically valid  

Organizers reserve the right to **withhold the Grand Prize** if independent verification cannot confirm the validity of a submission.

---

## 6. Important Clarification

This competition evaluates true reconstruction, not:

approximate similarity
statistical matching
plausible synthetic data generation

A submission that produces realistic-looking data but does not reconstruct the actual inputs will not qualify.

---

## 7. No-Winner Condition

If no submission satisfies all evaluation criteria:

**No Grand Prize will be awarded.**

---

## 8. Final Authority

All decisions are **final and binding**, including:
- Evaluation outcomes
- Threshold determination
- Prize eligibility
